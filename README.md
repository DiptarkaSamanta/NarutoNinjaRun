# 🍥 NARUTO: NINJA RUN (Shinobi Endless Runner)

An action-packed, fast-paced endless runner game inspired by the classic Chrome Dino run, infused with iconic Naruto Shippuden mechanics: playable characters, Jutsu abilities, Chakra management, Kawariami substitution logs, and epic multi-phase Boss Battles!

## 🎮 How to Play

### 🚀 Instant Launch
Double-click **`play.bat`** in the project folder to launch the game immediately in your browser!

Or open `index.html` directly in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Opera).

## 🥷 Playable Shinobi Roster

Each ninja has custom stats, passive traits, unique animations, and signature Jutsu:

### 1. 🍥 Naruto Uzumaki
- **Passive:** *Nine-Tails Vitality* - Slowly regenerates HP over time.
- **Jutsu 1 (K):** *Shadow Clone Dash* - Clones run ahead and absorb hits while clearing the lane.
- **Jutsu 2 (L):** *Rasengan* - Smashes forward in an invulnerable blue energy vortex destroying all obstacles.
- **Ultimate (U):** *Nine-Tails Cloak* - Unleashes crimson Kyuubi chakra shroud with extreme speed and invulnerability.

### 2. ⚡ Sasuke Uchiha
- **Passive:** *Sharingan Perception* - Basic shurikens pierce multiple enemies and obstacles.
- **Jutsu 1 (K):** *Fireball Jutsu (Katon)* - Hurls a massive rolling fireball down the lane.
- **Jutsu 2 (L):** *Chidori (One Thousand Birds)* - Electric lightning dash piercing through enemies and boss defenses.
- **Ultimate (U):** *Kirin Thunder* - Summons a celestial lightning dragon from the sky, wiping out all hazards.

### 3. 👁️ Kakashi Hatake (The Copy Ninja)
- **Passive:** *Tactical Genius* - Starts with +1 bonus Substitution Log and recovers logs faster.
- **Jutsu 1 (K):** *Kamui Warp* - Warps all nearby obstacles and hazards into another dimension.
- **Jutsu 2 (L):** *Lightning Blade (Raikiri)* - Swift cutting dash with piercing electric blade.
- **Ultimate (U):** *Kamui Shuriken Storm* - Rains dimensional shurikens that disintegrate bosses and traps.

### 4. 🌸 Sakura Haruno
- **Passive:** *Medical Kunoichi* - Ramen heals 50% more HP.
- **Jutsu 1 (K):** *Cherry Blossom Impact (Shanaro!)* - Slams the ground with monstrous chakra force, shattering all ground traps.
- **Jutsu 2 (L):** *Mystical Healing Palm* - Instantly restores +45 HP and activates a brief protective barrier.
- **Ultimate (U):** *Strength of a Hundred (Byakugou)* - Full HP restoration and temporary unstoppable powerhouse mode.

### 5. 🍃 Rock Lee
- **Passive:** *Leg Weights Dropped* - Highest jump velocity, quickest slide, and lowest stamina/chakra costs.
- **Jutsu 1 (K):** *Leaf Hurricane (Konoha Senpuu)* - Triple spinning flying kick clearing air and ground.
- **Jutsu 2 (L):** *Primary Lotus* - Slingshots high into the air and slams down with earth-shattering power.
- **Ultimate (U):** *Eight Gates Released* - Flaming emerald aura burst unleashing hyper-speed combos!

## 👹 Legendary Boss Battles

As your distance counter climbs, a flashing warning banner alerts you of approaching legendary foes:

1. **Zabuza Momochi (Demon of the Hidden Mist - ~700m)**:
   - Summons Hidden Mist to reduce visibility, unleashes giant Water Dragon waves and executioner sword strikes!
2. **Gaara of the Sand (~1,800m)**:
   - Deploys floating Sand Shields that absorb basic attacks, pelts you with Sand Shurikens, and summons sand storms.
3. **Orochimaru (~3,200m)**:
   - Summons Striking Shadow Snakes across multiple heights and launches poison senbon barrages.
4. **Pain / Deva Path (~5,000m)**:
   - Uses Shinra Tensei (Almighty Push) to repel attacks and launch deadly Black Receiver spikes.
5. **Madara Uchiha (~7,500m)**:
   - Deploys Susano'o ribcage armor, Majestic Destroyer Flame walls, and meteors!

Defeating bosses drops cascades of **Golden Ninja Scrolls** and grants huge bonus scores!

## 🍜 Items & Pickups

- **🍜 Ichiraku Ramen Bowl:** Restores 35 HP (50+ HP for Sakura).
- **📜 Ninja Scroll:** Collectable currency used in the Dojo to upgrade Max HP, Max Chakra, and Attack Damage.
- **🪵 Kawariami Substitution Log:** Automatic protection! When struck, your ninja turns into a wooden log with a smoke poof, completely nullifying damage.
- **🌀 Chakra Spirit Orb:** Instantly replenishes +45 Chakra Points.

---

## 🏯 Scroll Dojo & Upgrades

Spend scrolls gathered during your runs to permanently upgrade:
- **Ichiraku Feast:** Increases Maximum HP.
- **Chakra Meditation:** Increases Maximum Chakra capacity.
- **Taijutsu & Shuriken Mastery:** Increases damage dealt to bosses and obstacles.
- Progress automatically saves to your browser's local storage.

## 🎵 Dynamic Audio Engine

Built with a custom procedural **Web Audio API synthesizer**:
- Traditional Japanese pentatonic shamisen/koto melodies and fast taiko drum beats that scale with your running speed.
- Ominous war-horn and brass ostinato during Boss encounters.
- Authentic SFX: Shuriken metal clinks, Chidori 1,000 birds lightning crackle, Rasengan whirring energy vortex, Kawariami log poofs, and victory fanfares.
- Completely offline, self-contained, with zero external sound files to download.
